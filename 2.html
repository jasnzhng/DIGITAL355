Generally, when the code of online work is left open to examination, the work is subject not only to copying but alteration. So artists borrow or "steal" from each other; Vuk Cosic talks of how he, Heath Bunting, Alexei Shulgin, Olia Lialina, and Jodi, in effect, had neighboring studios, "like Picasso and Braque in Paris in 1907," so that each could see what the other was doing, respond to it, or collaborate.
